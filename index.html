Our Legacy · Since 1973

From one car‑hire desk to Bahrain’s heaviest fleet.

Alwardi began in 1973 as a modest car-hire and driving firm, founded by Mr. Abdulla Alwardi. Five decades later it is Bahrain’s largest heavy-transport and plant-hire company.

From our base in Sitra Industrial Area, we have moved the loads behind the Kingdom’s public and private landmark projects, building a reputation on solid management, careful planning and work delivered as promised.
